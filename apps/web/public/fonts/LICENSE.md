The OFL requires that the license accompany the fonts. All four faces above are
distributed under OFL-1.1; retain this notice when redistributing the WOFF2
assets. Both subsets are `latin` only — re-download from Google Fonts if the
product ever ships a language outside U+0000–00FF.
